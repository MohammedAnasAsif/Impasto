## What's new in 1.3.0

- **New colours:** midnight black throughout, electric blue for buttons, the selected filter and the size you've picked, and cantaloupe orange for prices, hearts and the bag count.
- **New app icon** in the same colours.
- **Dark status bar on Android** and a dark window on Windows, so nothing flashes white when the app starts.

All 30 wallpapers are still here, and new ones keep arriving without updates.

## Installing

- **Android:** open the APK and allow installs from your browser or file manager when asked. It installs over 1.2.0.
- **Windows:** the installers aren't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen will warn on first run. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
