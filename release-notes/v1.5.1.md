## What's new in 1.5.1

- **Mac app:** the full store with one-tap **Set as wallpaper** on every desktop, and **Auto-change** that keeps running from the menu bar and starts when you log in while it's on.
- **iPhone and iPad:** use the website. Open it in Safari and tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**. **Download PNG** opens the share sheet so you can choose **Save Image**, then set it from Photos with Share → **Use as Wallpaper**.
- Android and Windows are unchanged apart from the version number.

## Installing

- **Android:** open the APK and allow installs from your browser or file manager when asked. It installs over 1.5.0.
- **Windows:** the installers aren't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen will warn on first run. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Mac:** open the DMG and drag Impasto to Applications. The app isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first open is blocked: go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway**. The first time it sets a wallpaper, allow it to control *System Events*.
