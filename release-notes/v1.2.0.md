An open-source wallpaper store for Android and Windows. 30 wallpapers, every one a lossless PNG at your screen's exact size: 1440 × 3200 on phones, 3840 × 2160 on desktops. Free, with no accounts, ads or tracking.

## Download

| | File | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| **Android** | `Impasto-1.2.0.apk` | Android 10 or newer |
| **Windows** (installer) | `Impasto-Setup-1.2.0.exe` | Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit |
| **Windows** (no install) | `Impasto-1.2.0-portable.exe` | Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit |

## What's new in 1.2.0

- **7 new wallpapers:** Money Brain, Hush Money, Pay Your Bills, Vitamin Money, Supra Fund, The High Table and Royal Crest. They're made with ChatGPT and upscaled 4× to full size.
- **Money Brain** now leads the home page.
- **New wallpapers update themselves.** The apps read the catalog from this repo, so wallpapers added here appear without an app update.
- **Smaller download for new wallpapers.** These ones download their full file when you need it, which keeps the app small.

## Installing

- **Android:** open the APK and allow installs from your browser or file manager when asked. Version 1.2.0 installs over 1.1.0, since both are signed with the same key.
- **Windows:** the installers aren't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen will warn on first run. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
