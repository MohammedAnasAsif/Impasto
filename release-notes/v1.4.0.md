## What's new in 1.4.0

- **Auto-change, on both apps:** open **Library** and flip the **Auto-change** switch. You get a new wallpaper every minute, in store order, looping back to the first after the last. The Library page shows what's on now and a countdown to the next change.
- **Android:** choose **Home**, **Lock** or **Both** screens. It keeps going with the app closed and after a restart, and pauses while the screen is off to save battery. For changes exactly on the minute, tap **Allow exact timing** and switch on *Alarms & reminders* for Impasto.
- **Windows:** closing the window keeps Impasto running from the tray, with **Next wallpaper**, **Stop auto-change** and **Quit** in its menu. While Auto-change is on, Impasto starts with Windows, straight into the tray. It pauses while the PC is locked.
- Turn the switch off and everything goes back to normal: no tray icon, no starting with Windows, no background alarm.

## Installing

- **Android:** open the APK and allow installs from your browser or file manager when asked. It installs over 1.3.2.
- **Windows:** the installers aren't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen will warn on first run. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
