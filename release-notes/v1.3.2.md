## What's new in 1.3.2

- **Android:** the earlier layout is back, with the big featured wallpaper at the top, bold sans-serif headings and the same midnight black, electric blue and cantaloupe colours. The new logo stays.
- **Windows:** unchanged, still the light-beam design from 1.3.0.

## Installing

- **Android:** open the APK and allow installs from your browser or file manager when asked. It installs over 1.3.1.
- **Windows:** the installers aren't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen will warn on first run. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
