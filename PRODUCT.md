# Impasto — product context

Inferred from the founding brief (no interview was run; assumptions are marked).

- **What it is:** an open-source wallpaper store for Android and Windows. Every wallpaper is thick
  oil-paint art shipped as a lossless PNG at the device's native size.
- **Who it's for:** people who want their phone and desktop to look like the painted wallpapers
  trending on Instagram, without compressed reposts. (Assumption: mostly 16–30, mobile-first.)
- **The one job:** find a wallpaper, then download it or set it in one tap.
- **Shape:** e-commerce patterns (shop, collections, product page, bag, checkout, receipt) applied to
  free downloads. Price is always "Free".
- **Content source:** a `catalog.json` in this repo. The apps bundle the starter pack and can read a
  remote catalog so new wallpapers ship without an app update.
- **Mode:** Experience for browsing (the art leads, chrome recedes), Operate for bag and library.
- **Voice:** short, confident, a little dry. Never hustle-bro.
